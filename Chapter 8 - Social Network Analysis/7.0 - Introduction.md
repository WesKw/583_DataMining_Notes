- Early search engines use content similarity, via [[Information Retrieval and Web Search]] methods (cosine, TF-IDF)
- But content similarity was no longer sufficient
	- Too many pages
	- Content similarity is easily spammed
- Researchers resorted to hyperlinks
	- Use words found in hyperlinks
	- Hyperlinks connect web pages
		- Some hyperlinks organize information at the same site, others point to pages from other web sites (implicit conveyance of authority to pages being pointed to)
		- A page pointed to by many other pages -> likely to have a good amount of authority on a particular subject matter
	- 2 hyperlink-based search algorithms: PageRank and HITS
		- Both related to social networks, exploit hyperlinks according to levels or prestige or authority
	- HITS -> Jon Kleinberg
	- [[7.3 - PageRank]] powers google, Sergey Brin and Larry Page
- Hyperlinks also useful for finding web communities
- Web community - cluster of densely linked pages representing a group of people w/ a special interest
- What is **Social Network Analysis**? 
	- Study of social entities (**actor**s) and their interactions and relationships
	- Can be represented w/ a network or graph
		- Study network structure
		- role, position, prestige of actors
	- Page: actor
	- Hyperlink: relationship
