- [[7.3 - PageRank]] just deals with ranks
- What if we want more than just rank
- Consider research publications:
	- Surveys summarizing a lot of stuff
	- Seminal papers with original ideas
	- Both of these get a shitttttload of citations
- HITS - Hypertext Induced Topic Search
	- Designed for search
- Search query dependent
- When a user issues a search query:
	- 1) HITS first expands the list of relevant pages returned by a search engine
	- 2) Produces two rankings of the expanded set of pages **[[7.4.1 - authority ranking]]** and **[[7.4.2 - hub ranking]]**
- **A good hub points to many good authorities**
- **A good authority is pointed to by many good hubs**
- **Mutual reinforcement relationship** b/w authorities and hubs, we can represent this as a bipartite sub-graph

- ## Grab pages
	- Given a broad search query $q$, HITS collects a set of pages as follows:
		- 1) Send $q$ to the search engine
		- 2) Collects $t$ (200 in HITS paper) highest ranked pages, called the **root set** $W$
		- 3) Grow $W$ by including any pages pointed to by a page in $W$ and any page that points to a page in $W$, which gives the **base set** $S$
- ## Link Graph G
	- HITS works on the pages in $S$ and assigns all pages in $S$ an [[7.4.1 - authority ranking]] (score) and [[7.4.2 - hub ranking]] (score).
	- $n$ pages in $S$
	- Use $G=(V,E)$ to denote the hyperlink graph of $S$
	- Use $L$ to denote the adjacency matrix 
- Authority score is the sum of all of the hub scores$$a(i) = \sum_{(j,i)\in E}{h(j)}$$
- Hub score is the sum of the authority scores$$h(i)=\sum_{(i,j)\in E}{a(i)}$$
- Matrix form:
	- $a$ denotes the column vector w/ all authority scores
	- $h$ denotes the column vector w/ all hub scores
	- Then: $a=L^Th$ and $h=La$
- Computation of scores is the same as the computation of the [[7.3 - PageRank]] scores, using power iteration
	- Iterations for generating final solutions are:
	  $a_k = L^Ta_{k-1}$
	  $h_k=LL^Th_{k-1}$
	  with $a_0=h_0=(1, 1, 1, ..., 1)$
- Problems: 
	- This does not satisfy the conditions for the Markov Chain
	- Also $a$ never converges
	- Most of the time the algorithm does converge, but in some cases it does not

- Strengths of HITS
	- Can provide more relevant authority and hub pages by ranking via query topic
- What are the issues with HITS?
	- Inefficient at query time
		- Collecting root set, expanding it, and eigenvector computations are all very expensive
	- It's easily spammed - we have authority over hub pages and hub score is connected to authority score - we have indirect control
	- [[Concept drift]]
	- Smaller topics are much more difficult to work with.
