- Also called **opinion mining**
- Opinions influence behaviors
	- Chatbots, AI
	- Social media opinion data
- **Sentiment** - Attitude, thought, or judgement prompted by a feeling
- **Opinion** - A view, judgement, or appraisal formed in the mind about a particular matter
- Intellectually challenging, many applications
- Semantic analysis problem
- LLMs are very useful
- difficult problem
- large and active research area
- 2 Opinion types
	- [[Regular Opinion]]
	- [[Comparative Opinion]]
	- Focus on regular opinions, just call them opinions
- An opinion is a quadruple
	- (target, sentiment, holder, time)
	- Targets can be very complex
		- "I bought an iPhone. The voice quality is amazing"
		- Is target the voice quality? Not exactly
- Better... an opinion is a quintuple
	- (entity, aspect, sentiment, holder, time)
	- [[entity]] - target entity or object
	- [[aspect]] - feature of the entity
	- [[sentiment]] - positive, negative, neutral, or emotion
	- [[holder]] - opinion holder
	- [[time]] - when the opinion was expressed
	- Aspect-based sentiment analysis
	- We can get lots of quintuples from reviews for example
